1. Information stored in the app

SingKey does not require an account. Recent songs, playlist names, saved song titles, video and thumbnail URLs, and semitone settings are stored in the app's private space on your device. Your last “Prefer backing tracks” setting and home song group are also stored on the device. This information is not sent to the operator's servers. The app does not use cloud sync or Android automatic backup. Suggestions and thumbnails are temporarily cached in memory while the app runs. Chart song titles, artists, chart periods and refresh times are stored on the device for offline display.

App language and whether recent playback is recorded are also stored on the device. Turning recording off stops adding new history. Existing history can be cleared separately in Settings without changing your library.

When you choose to back up your library, playlist names, song titles, video and thumbnail URLs, and saved keys are exported to a document location you select. Audio, video files and recent history are not included. Backups are not encrypted and can be read by anyone with access to that location. A cloud document provider may transfer and store the file if you select that provider. Restore reads a file you select and adds its playlists to your library. Exported files survive app removal; delete them from the chosen storage location when no longer needed.

2. Connections to external services

Search, suggestions, video playback and thumbnails connect directly to Google/YouTube and the relevant content servers. When you pause typing, suggestions may send part or all of the query before you press Search. Requests may include the query, selected video identifiers, and language/country settings corresponding to the app language. With backing search enabled, a localized backing keyword may be added to the query. Your IP address and request headers are transmitted in the course of internet communication. External services handle and retain information under their own policies.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en

Refreshing Korean songs connects directly to TJ Media's public chart; refreshing Japanese songs connects to the JOYSOUND (XING) public chart. Requests include the chart type, period or public page address, but not your personal queries, playback history or playlists. Your IP address and request headers are sent to the respective servers. Pop recommendations are included in the app and require no chart request to display. Tapping a source or reference catalog link opens that website in an external browser.

TJ Media Privacy Policy: https://www.tjmedia.co.kr/terms?cate_cd=T01

JOYSOUND (XING) Privacy Policy: https://xing.co.jp/privacy

3. Optional usage analytics and advertising

Analytics is off by default. Only after you agree in the notice or Settings does the app send events over HTTPS to PostHog, Inc. using its EU ingestion service in Frankfurt, Germany. Previous Firebase consent is not reused for this service. The purpose is to improve features and reliability.

Events contain app screens, search success/failure and result counts, actual practice time, pitch/save/queue actions and reuse of saved songs, app version/language/orientation, timestamps and a randomly generated installation identifier. We do not include search text, song titles, video URLs, playlist names, email or voice. We do not use login identities or advertising IDs, record screens, automatically capture clicks or personalize advertising.

Internet requests transmit IP addresses and request headers. Analytics IP geolocation is disabled and the project is configured to discard client IP addresses from stored events. Service/security processing and international processing follow PostHog's privacy and subprocessor policies.

All practice features work if you decline. Turning analytics off stops new transmissions, cancels in-flight requests, and removes the local analytics identifier and unsent queue. It does not immediately delete records already received by the service. Unsent events are bounded and kept only in memory; some may be lost offline or when the app closes.

The free plan provides one year of event data retention; this is not a guarantee of automatic deletion exactly one year later. Contact getbathtime@gmail.com for access or deletion requests. Without a login identity, an email alone may not locate past events. We will explain what can be located and the available deletion procedure. Retention and deletion follow the service's actual policies and procedures.

PostHog Privacy Policy: https://posthog.com/privacy

Data storage and deletion: https://posthog.com/docs/privacy/data-storage

This distribution does not include Google advertising, UMP or Firebase Analytics SDKs and does not display or request ads. Before adding ads we will update this policy and any required consent notices.

4. Retention and deletion

You can delete saved songs and recent history within the app, or clear SingKey's storage/data in Android settings. Uninstalling also removes the app's data from your device. Clearing all data or uninstalling can erase saved playlists and should be your own choice. For information independently processed by external services, use those services' rights-request procedures.

5. Support inquiries

The contact screen only displays the support email address (getbathtime@gmail.com). The in-app form and automatic sending are disabled. Tapping the address lets you compose and send an email yourself in an external email app. SingKey does not automatically attach app or Android versions, playback history, your library or device identifiers.

The operator uses the email address and message you send to handle and answer your inquiry. Your chosen email service and the operator’s Gmail service process and store the email under their respective privacy policies.

Do not send passwords, identity documents or payment details. To request access, correction or deletion, contact the same support address. The operator deletes inquiry emails when the response and follow-up are complete, except where a legal retention obligation applies. Sending an inquiry is optional; search, playback and your library work without it.

6. Protection and contact

External app requests use HTTPS. Locally stored information is kept in the app's private storage. Send privacy questions or rights requests to BathTime Studio (getbathtime@gmail.com).

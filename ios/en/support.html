SingKey Help

Find a backing track, practice in a comfortable key, and remember that key for next time.

Get started

1. Find a song

Search by song or artist. Backing-track search adds a karaoke keyword for your app language.

2. Adjust your key

Use the − / + buttons during playback to move in semitone steps from -12 to +12 while keeping the tempo.

3. Save it for next time

Save a song and its key to a library list. Queue more songs when you want to keep singing.

Common questions

Does this remove vocals or melody?

No. Backing-track search adjusts the search query; it does not separate vocals or melody from the original recording.

What if a video will not play?

Check your connection and try searching again. Private, removed, restricted videos or external service changes can prevent playback. Playback pauses when you leave the app.

How do I move my library?

Export a library backup in Settings, then import that file on your other device. Import adds new lists without deleting your existing songs. Backups contain no media and are not encrypted; store them carefully.

Can I decline analytics?

Yes. You can turn usage analytics on or off in Settings without losing practice features. Advertising privacy choices are separate and appear in Settings when required.

Why did a shared link not open SingKey?

Select SingKey in the share menu and finish the share action, then open SingKey yourself. The received link opens a search result without autoplay.

Does clearing history delete saved songs?

No. Clearing recent playback history leaves your library songs and saved keys intact.
